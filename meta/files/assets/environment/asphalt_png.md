# Cracked Road Asphalt Tile

FILE: assets/environment/asphalt.png
DESCRIPTION: Seamless top-down tile of broken wet asphalt for the street that runs along the top of the yard.
UTILITY: ideogram
MODE: tiling
TILING_MODE: both
WIDTH: 1024
HEIGHT: 1024
OUTPUT_FORMAT: png
RENDERING_SPEED: BALANCED
USAGE: Canvas street layer of the First Night scenario, repeated horizontally along the roadway.
PROMPT: |
  Seamless tileable top-down texture of abandoned wet asphalt road at night, photorealistic.
  Material: near-black wet tarmac, spider-web cracks, small potholes patched with gravel, dark oil stains, thin reflective wet sheen catching faint warm light.
  Palette: deep charcoal-brown blacks (#0d0c0a, #1a1712), subtle warm amber reflections in the wet sheen, hints of cold blue-grey only in the deepest cracks; rich low-key values, no flat mid-grey.
  Mood: rain-soaked night street for a survival horror game, dark and glossy.
  Flat even lighting, orthographic top-down, no road markings, no cast shadows, no objects, no characters, no text, no watermark, no border.

COMMENTS: ## Design Notes
- Road markings are drawn procedurally in Canvas, so the texture must stay clean of painted lines.
- Slightly colder than the yard ground so the street reads as a separate surface.
