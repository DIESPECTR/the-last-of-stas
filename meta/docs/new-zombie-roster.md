## Production rules

- One identity appears at most once in a wave.
- Crowd identities share animation families; special units receive bespoke attack behavior.
- Character recognition comes from face, hair, glasses, clothing and silhouette—not text labels.
- Zombie treatment stays stylized and non-graphic: pallor, grime, veins, torn clothing and bruising; no exposed organs.
- Cat and dog remain visually recognizable and attached to their owner units.
- `injured_kuok` preserves the crutches, cast and bruised face in every direction.
- `lilliput_zombie` uses a genuinely smaller runtime scale and hitbox, not merely a distant-looking sprite.
- `tattooed_crowd_zombie` preserves the long dark curls, pale wrap top and readable black chest tattoo, especially in front-facing frames.
